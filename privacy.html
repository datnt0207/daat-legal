Privacy Policy

Daat for iPhone · Effective 9 November 2026

Daat turns photos you choose into photo strips and cards. This policy explains what the app collects and what it doesn't. In short: your photos never leave your iPhone, there is no account, and the only data we receive is usage statistics and crash reports that are not linked to your identity.

Who we are

Daat is published by an individual developer, shown as the seller on Daat's App Store page ("we", "us"), who is responsible for the data described here. Contact: daat.photo@gmail.com.

Your photos

  • You pick photos with the iOS photo picker. Daat sees only the photos you pick, never your whole library.
  • Photos are placed into templates and rendered on your device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • When you save a finished image, iOS asks for permission to add photos to your library. Daat uses this only to save the image; it does not read your library.
  • When you share an image, the iOS share sheet sends it to the app you choose. That app's own privacy policy then applies.

Reminders

When you tap "Remind me" on an upcoming seasonal collection, the reminder is scheduled on your iPhone; no notification server is involved. The fact that you asked to be reminded about that collection is recorded in the usage statistics below. You can turn reminders off in Daat's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To learn which templates people like and to fix bugs, Daat uses Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They collect:

  • App usage events: opening the app, which screen is shown, viewing and using a template (its ID, style and collection), how many photos you picked, which editing tools you used, finishing an export (its format), tapping Share (not where you share to), and asking for a seasonal reminder. Firebase also records standard events such as first open, sessions, time in the app and app or iOS updates, plus the device language. Never the photos themselves.
  • A random app-install identifier created by Firebase, so events from one install can be counted together. It is not your name, email, phone number or Apple ID, and a new one is created if you delete and reinstall the app.
  • Approximate location (country or city level), derived by Google from your IP address. Daat does not use Location Services.
  • Device and crash information, such as the iPhone model, iOS version, app version and, if the app crashes, a technical report of where it crashed.

This data is not linked to your identity. We do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not ask to track you, and we do not use this data for advertising or share it with data brokers. We never sell personal data.

Why: we rely on our legitimate interest in understanding how the app is used and keeping it working, with the data kept to the minimum above. You can turn usage statistics off at any time in Daat's Settings (Share usage statistics); nothing more is sent from then on. Crash reports stay on so we can fix crashes.

Who processes it: Google processes this data on our behalf under Google's data processing terms for Firebase and Google Analytics, with Google's own data-sharing options turned off, and may process it in the United States and other countries under the safeguards in those terms (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses). See Privacy and Security in Firebase and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

How long: event-level usage data is kept for no longer than 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, then deleted. Aggregated reports, which don't identify any install, may be kept longer.

What we don't collect

No account, name, email, phone number, contacts, payment details, messages or browsing history. Daat has no in-app purchases today.

Children

Daat is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age for consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

  • Deleting Daat removes the data it keeps inside the app on your iPhone and starts a new install identifier if you reinstall. Images you saved to Photos or shared stay where you put them, and statistics already sent are deleted on the schedule above (Firebase removes an unused install identifier within 180 days).
  • Depending on where you live (for example Vietnam, the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, and to object to its use for usage statistics (the Settings switch above does this at once). Because the data isn't linked to your identity we usually can't find it from your name or email, but write to us and we'll help as far as we can. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
